# Step 2 Notes

## Platforms

### Landsat

Pros:

- years: full coverage
- easily acquired
- good spectral coverage

Cons:

- poor resolution (30m)
- dates might be elusive

### Sentinel

Pros:

- decent resolution (10-20m)
- good spectral coverage
- better on dates than Landsat

Cons:

- not available until 2016

### NAIP

Pros:

- great resolution (1m or less)
- covers all years

Cons:

- no choice on dates (but generally available for the growing season)
- poor spectral coverage (only vis + NIR)
- likely not be available for all dates for all locations
